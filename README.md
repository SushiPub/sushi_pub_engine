# 🍣 SushiPub  
### SocialPublisher for AI-First Content & Design Teams

**SushiPub** (short for **SocialPublisher**) is a **cloud-only, AI-native publishing platform** built for modern media teams, editors, and UI developers.

It combines **editorial AI**, **builder AI**, and **governed publishing workflows** into a single platform — with **no local development**, **staging-first testing**, and **full human control**.

---

## ✨ What Is SushiPub?

SushiPub is a **social publishing and experience management platform** where:

- ✍️ **Editors** use AI to draft, optimize, and publish content  
- 🧱 **Developers & designers** use a Replit-like **Builder AI** to evolve UI and layouts  
- 🤖 **AI assists**, but **never bypasses approvals**  
- ☁️ Everything runs **100% online**, from design to staging to production  

SushiPub is built for **real production environments**, not experiments.

---

## 🎯 Core Philosophy

> **AI should accelerate work — not bypass governance.**

SushiPub is intentionally:
- ✅ AI-assisted  
- ✅ Cloud-only  
- ✅ Role-based  
- ✅ Audit-friendly  

And intentionally **not**:
- ❌ A free-form IDE  
- ❌ A one-click AI publisher  
- ❌ A local-dev-based platform  

---

## 🧠 Two AI Workspaces, One Platform

### ✍️ Editor AI (Editors & Journalists)

Editor AI supports editorial teams with:

- AI-assisted article drafting  
- Headline and SEO suggestions  
- Content summaries for social media  
- Multi-format publishing (web, mobile, social)  
- Full revision history and approvals  

> AI suggests. Editors decide. Nothing is published automatically.

---

### 🧱 Builder AI (Governed, Replit-like)

Builder AI is a **cloud-based AI IDE for UI evolution**, not a general coding sandbox.

It allows developers and designers to:

- Modify layouts and components visually  
- Use AI to suggest structural or design changes  
- Preview changes instantly in staging  
- Work without local machines or tooling  

**By design, Builder AI does NOT allow:**
- Backend access  
- Secrets access  
- Arbitrary code execution  
- Direct production deployment  

All edits are **schema-driven and component-based**.
